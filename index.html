R² Photobooth

An offline Android app that turns a tablet, a camera and a photo printer into a self-service photobooth for events.

  • Controls a Sony α6400 or Canon PowerShot G7 X Mark III over USB, with live view on the tablet
  • Prints on the spot on a Canon SELPHY, over USB or Wi-Fi
  • Guests retake, rearrange, reframe and filter their photos before printing
  • Custom event designs: upload a PNG frame and the app finds the photo windows
  • QR code digital copies on a guest gallery that cleans itself up after 3 days
  • A short recap video of every session, made on the tablet
  • Keeps working offline and uploads when the internet is back

R² Booking System

The online booking site for R² Production Studios. Clients check availability and request photography and videography coverage, camera rentals and photobooth packages.

  • Live availability for coverage, rentals and photobooth packages
  • Camera rentals with pickup times and refundable deposits
  • Every request gets a reference number; the team reviews it and confirms by email
  • Admin dashboard: bookings, calendar views, blocked dates, packages, camera inventory, portfolio, notifications and settings
  • Printing and photocopying requests handled through Messenger
